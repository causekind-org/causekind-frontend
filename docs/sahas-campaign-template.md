# Sahas campaign brief (template)

Fill this in and hand it to Claude with: "Draft this Sahas campaign in the admin panel."
Claude fills the forms in the browser and never clicks Save, Publish, Pause, Close or Cancel.
You log in yourself and save everything yourself.

Write "skip" for anything you don't know. Claude leaves that part empty and never invents
numbers, dates or claims, because everything here becomes public.

## 4. How Claude works through it
1. Opens the admin URL and waits for you to log in. It never asks for your password.
2. Tells you the URL and whether it is staging or production before changing anything.
3. Details tab: fills the fields and stops. You click Save, which creates the draft.
4. Page content tab: tells you which fields already have content, clicks "Fill empty sections
   with starter text", fills your values, uploads photos and videos in order with captions, and
   checks the starter text for claims you haven't confirmed (80G, stats). Stays on that tab
   until you click "Save page content", because switching tabs can lose unsaved uploads.
5. Ends with what it filled, what it skipped and any upload errors.
